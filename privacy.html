Prism Case · Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Applies to the Prism Case iOS app and this website

Prism Case includes four offline detective cases. No account is required. Live AI generation and advertising are inactive in this release.

1. Local data

Case progress and acknowledgement of the privacy notice are stored on your device. You can restart a case in the game or delete the app to remove its local app storage.

2. Network and AI

The four cases require no network connection. This release does not offer AI generation, request API keys, or send requests to AI providers. We operate no game server, analytics service or crash-reporting service.

3. Advertising and tracking

Advertising is currently inactive. Although the app includes Google AdMob integration for future use, no ad configuration endpoint is set, and the app does not initialise the ad SDK, request ads or request tracking permission.

Before a future release enables advertising, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated. Tracking that requires Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission will only occur after permission is granted. You can decline and continue playing.

4. First-launch notice

The Privacy & Data Use notice explains local storage and potential future advertising. Continue acknowledges that you have read the notice; it does not grant tracking permission. You can reopen it in Settings.

5. Audience

The game contains fictional murder mysteries and textual descriptions of crime scenes. Please refer to the age rating displayed on its App Store product page.

6. Retention and deletion

We do not receive your game progress. It remains in the app's local storage until you reset or remove it. Data held in older development builds, including any previously entered API key in the iOS Keychain, is not accessed or transmitted by the disabled AI feature; deleting an app does not guarantee deletion of Keychain entries.

7. Changes and contact

This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. Contact email will be added before public release.
